    💡 How Zoot Games Works

    Everything you need to know to start playing and earning

    1

    Connect Your Phantom Wallet

    Click "Connect & Start Betting" on the home screen. Your Phantom wallet will ask you to approve the connection. This links your Solana wallet to the platform — we never access your private keys.

    2

    Deposit SOL to Your Balance

    In the lobby, use the wallet panel to deposit SOL into your Zoot Games balance. Your funds are held in a secure escrow smart contract on Solana. You can withdraw anytime.

    3

    Pick a Game & Create a Bet

    Choose from 24 games: Domino, Chess, Checkers, Tic Tac Toe, Mancala, Connect Four, Rock Paper Scissors, Coin Flip, Dice, Battleship, Memory, Nim, Hex, Math Duel, Reaction Time, and many more. Set your wager amount in SOL and create a challenge.

    4

    Play Against a Real Opponent

    Another player sees your open challenge and accepts it by matching your wager. The game starts instantly — both players have their SOL locked in escrow until the game ends. No bots, only real players.

    5

    Win & Get Paid Instantly

    The winner receives 1.8x their wager directly to their balance. If you bet 1 SOL and win, you get 1.8 SOL back. Payouts are instant and can be withdrawn to your wallet at any time.

    ⚽ Sports Betting
    1

    Bet on Live Sports Events

    Switch to the Sports section from the menu. Browse live events across Soccer, Football, NBA, Baseball, Hockey, MMA, and more. Pick a team (or draw), set your SOL amount, and place your bet through Phantom Wallet.

    2

    Another Player Takes the Other Side

    Your bet appears in the "Open Sports Bets" section. Another player who disagrees with your pick accepts it by wagering the same amount. Once matched, both sides are locked in.

    3

    Event Ends — Winner Takes the Pot

    When the sports event finishes, the player who picked the winning team receives 1.8x payout. Same instant payout system as games.
