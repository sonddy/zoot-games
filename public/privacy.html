Privacy Policy

Zoot Games (zootgames.org) — Last updated: August 2, 2026

This policy explains what information Zoot Games collects when you use our website, our Telegram Mini App (@ZootGamesBot), or our mobile app, and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Telegram profile data. If you open Zoot Games through Telegram, we receive the basic profile data Telegram shares with Mini Apps: your Telegram user ID, first name, and username (if set). We verify this data cryptographically but do not access your contacts, messages, or phone number.
  • Wallet address. If you connect a Solana wallet, we store your public wallet address to match you into games and send payouts. We never receive or store your private keys or seed phrase.
  • Game and bet activity. Matches played, bets placed, results, and payout records, tied to your wallet address or Telegram ID.
  • Technical data. IP address and basic connection metadata, used for fraud and abuse prevention and to operate the service.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • No private keys, seed phrases, or wallet signing power.
  • No Telegram messages, contacts, or phone numbers.
  • No payment card or bank details — all wagers are on-chain transactions you sign yourself in your own wallet.

3. How we use your information

  • To run matches, hold wagers in escrow, and pay winners.
  • To prevent cheating, abuse, and duplicate-payout fraud.
  • To operate, debug, and improve the platform.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Blockchain transparency

Wagers and payouts are transactions on the public Solana blockchain. Anyone can see them, and they cannot be edited or deleted by us or anyone else. Your wallet address and its transaction history are public by the nature of blockchains.

5. Services we rely on

  • Telegram — bot and Mini App platform (their privacy policy).
  • Solana network — public blockchain used for wagers and payouts.
  • Phantom — wallet you may choose to connect (their privacy policy).
  • Render — server hosting.
  • Google Firebase — game-state and account persistence.

6. Data retention

Game and bet records are kept as long as needed to operate the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete data tied to your Telegram account or wallet where the law allows; on-chain records cannot be deleted.

7. Age requirement

Zoot Games involves real-money wagering. You must be 18 or older (or the age of majority where you live) to play. Play responsibly.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version always lives at zootgames.org/privacy.html.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: message us in Telegram at @ZootGamesBot.
